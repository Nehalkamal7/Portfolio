Student: How do transformer attention heads calculate the Query, Key, and Value alignments?
Seba AI Tutor:
Think of Q, K, and V as relational lookups in a high-dimensional vector space. The attention score is derived by computing:
Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( (Q · KT) / √dk ) · V
The dot product quantifies semantic relevance, scaled by √dk to maintain stable gradients before weighting value vectors.
